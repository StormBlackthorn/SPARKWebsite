Our Mission

SPARK's mission is to introduce STEM to children who are interested and provide them with opportunities to interact with technologies in a fun and engaging way.

Additionally, SPARK aims to bring attention to all aspects of STEM, in a world where many emphasize the sole importance of coding and programming.

Our Vision

SPARK's vision is to have a world where younger people have the ability to pursue their passion for STEM, changing the world one innovation at a time.

We Believe That:

  • Children should be taught in the way they learn best.
  • Barriers like poverty, underfunded schools, and systemic inequities often limit access to STEM opportunities—and we aim to change that.
  • One's background shouldn't hinder their interests, future, and dreams.
  • Young people can and will make an impact on their community.
  • A good mentor can change someone's future.
