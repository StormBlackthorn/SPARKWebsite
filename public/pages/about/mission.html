About SPARK

About Us

We are a student-run organization that brings hands-on STEM education to local elementary schools through clubs, projects, and mentorship.

Our Mission

SPARK introduces STEM through fun, engaging lessons and hands-on projects. We want every student to see themselves as a creator, not just a consumer of technology. Our volunteers tailor lessons to each classroom so every kid can build, test, and ask questions with confidence.

Fully Ran by Students

We design lessons, run clubs, and mentor students ourselves. Every session is built by high school volunteers who care deeply about access and opportunity. Students plan activities, iterate on projects, and keep improving each session so kids stay excited to learn.
