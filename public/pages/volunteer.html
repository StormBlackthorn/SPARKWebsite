Volunteer

Through countless hours of hard work put together by both student and parent volunteers, we have been able to make a real difference in our community. Join us and help us expand our impact and brighten our future!

We want you to join us!

Everyone can make a difference, and we need all the help that we can get. Check out the section below and see what you can do!

*Disclaimer: You must be a high school student to volunteer with SPARK.

Step 1: Find the chapter you want to join

Visit the Chapters page to see the list of existing chapters. Find a location that works for you with lesson subjects that fits your interest.

Step 2: Reach out

Contact us and express your interest in joining the chapter.

And you're done! We will be in touch with you shortly.

*Disclaimer: Our volunteer policy is that you can only miss up to two sessions per season, unless it is for some unforeseen circumstance. Moreover, if you need to skip a session, please tell your local club leader.

Step 1: Contact us

Contact us and express your interest in starting a chapter. We can provide help and our experiences along the way to ensure your success.

Step 2: Gather some friends

Find other people who are also interested and willing to help you start the chapter. We recommend at least having 4-5 founding members.

Step 3: Come up with a plan

Brainstorm what you guys will teach and how you will teach it. Come up with a basic curriculum.

Step 4: Reach out to your local schools

Email your local school and ask for their interests in starting a chapter. Do be warned that this part could be tricky and time consuming as school administrators are often busy.

Step 5: Start the club!

Once you have the necessary approvals and support, you can officially start teaching afterschool clubs at your selected elementary! Have fun, learn, gain experience, and repeat!

We are always in need of chaperons!

Often, we have to cut down on the sizes of our clubs or even cancel them altogether due to a lack of adult supervision. If you are a parent or guardian interested in helping out, please sign up to be a chaperon.

The process is different for every school, so please consult your local school's website for more information how to become a chaperon.

Most of the times, chaperons are only responsible for taking attendance. During lessons, chaperons are free to do whatever as long as they stay in the class.

*Note: Most school require adult volunteers to be ran through a background check, which may require additional charges.
