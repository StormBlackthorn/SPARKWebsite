Why volunteer with SPARK?

High school students lead afterschool STEM clubs at local elementary schools. You'll plan lessons, mentor kids, and build real leadership experience — usually about 2–3 hours per week during the school year.

  • Teach hands-on STEM through projects kids actually enjoy
  • Develop leadership, communication, and teaching skills
  • Earn service hours while making a direct impact in your community
  • Join a network of student volunteers across 30+ schools
