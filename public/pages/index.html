SPARK

STEM Path Activities & Resources for Kids

A fully student-run organization dedicated to providing afterschool teaching for STEM related subjects in local elementary schools.

We believe that everyone deserves the chance of STEM education.

In our current era, where technology is everywhere in our basic, everyday lives, we at SPARK believe that all children deserve the chance to learn about STEM, regardless of economic or social status. We provide affordable and accessible resources, activities, and lessons to make sure that every children gets an equal chance to learn about what shapes our everyday world.
